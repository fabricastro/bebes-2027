# Surface brief: public wish page

Scope: `public/index.html` (public form). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: women wishing to conceive, arriving from Instagram on a phone. Action: submit name + birth date. Proof/content: the campaign copy only, plus the live participant count. Constraints: no public list; symbolic, never medical; Spanish (voseo). Admin panel (`public/admin.html`) is Operate and inherits the world quietly.

Open: doctor's display name / handle (configurable via `SIGNATURE` var).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each wish is a satin ribbon with the name hot-stamped in gold, tied into one long ribbon that grows with every participant. Refuses the pastel-watercolor-flowers baby-shower card.

OWN-WORLD: Flat satin red ground (#A80F29, deep #6E0919 for the closing band), flat gold (#EBCA7E) type, no imitated foil/emboss/sheen; a full-bleed white cotton band with a dashed gold selvedge stitch carries the form; a gold safety pin glyph as the only ornament. Type: Literata caps in gold (souvenir-ribbon stamping face; user asked on 2026-11-19 to replace Bodoni with a more legible face: "la fuente bodoni cambiala por una mas legible"), Figtree for body. No flowers, no illustrations, no emoji in the UI.

STORY: She reads the wish in gold, understands she is joining a chain, sees how many already joined, writes her name and sees it stamped live on the ribbon, submits, and the ribbon is pinned to the chain.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed red. Title "Bebés 2027" in large gold caps (adaptation: project name supplied by the user after the contract, PRODUCT.md); no kicker (craft-floor ban); "Un deseo, una esperanza, una nueva vida" heads the motive section. A white ribbon band crosses edge to edge below: it is the form (name, date) with the live gold-stamped preview of the name; primary button "Sumar mi deseo" closes the band. Under it the chain: one anonymous ribbon piece per wish, then "Ya somos N".

FORM: Cinta roja, position 3 on the ordered list; seed key c826bdc1. Signature interaction: live hot-stamp preview; on submit the ribbon closes with a safety pin and slides into the chain.
